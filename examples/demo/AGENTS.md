# This is a solid-fuse app

solid-fuse and SolidJS 2.0 are both new and may not match your training data. Before writing any code:

- **solid-fuse**: read the relevant doc under `node_modules/solid-fuse/dist/docs/`
- **SolidJS 2.0**: read the relevant doc under `node_modules/solid-fuse/dist/docs/solid-2.0/` — it has breaking changes vs the Solid 1.x docs in your training data (mirrored from https://github.com/solidjs/solid/tree/next/documentation/solid-2.0)

## Check your work in the running app

Start `fuse dev` in the background (`fuse dev --headless` on a machine with no screen), wait for "Ready for fuse drive", then tap through the app and take screenshots with `fuse drive` (`fuse drive help-ai` lists the commands). Docs: `node_modules/solid-fuse/dist/docs/agents/`.
