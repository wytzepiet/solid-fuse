A settings screen. It has a title "Settings" at the top and two labelled groups below it. Group "Notifications" has three rows (Push notifications, Email digest, Sounds); each row is a label on the left and an on/off toggle on the right, and each toggle keeps its own state. Group "Display" has a row "Text size" with a slider ranging from 12 to 24 whose current value is shown as a whole number at the right end of the label line, and a "Dark mode" toggle row. Each group is a white rounded card with a small uppercase grey group heading above it. The whole screen scrolls when the content does not fit. Export a component named `SettingsScreen` (no props).
