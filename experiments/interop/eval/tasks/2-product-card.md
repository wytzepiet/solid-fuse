A product card, 180 wide. On top a square product image loaded from a URL, filling the card's width, cropped to fill. Below it, inside 12 of padding: the product title (16, semi-bold, at most two lines, truncated with an ellipsis), then a line with the price in bold on the left and, on the right, a small star icon followed by the rating number. The card has a white background, corners rounded by 16 (the image's top corners must follow the rounding) and a soft drop shadow. Export a component named `ProductCard` with props `{ imageUrl: string; title: string; price: number; rating: number }`; the price is shown with a dollar sign and two decimals.
