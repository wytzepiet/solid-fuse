A scrollable chat thread rendered from `props.messages: { id: number; text: string; time: string; mine: boolean }[]`. Each message is a bubble at most 260 wide that is only as wide as its text needs. My messages sit against the right edge with a blue background and white text; the others sit against the left edge with a light grey background and dark text. Bubbles have corners rounded by 16, except that the bottom corner on the sender's side is rounded by only 4. Under each bubble a tiny grey timestamp is aligned to the same side as the bubble. There is 8 of vertical space between messages and 12 of padding around the whole list. Export a component named `ChatThread`.
