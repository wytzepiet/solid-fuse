A collapsible panel. Its header is a row with a title on the left and a chevron icon on the right; tapping anywhere on the header toggles the body. The body is a paragraph of text inside 16 of padding. Opening and closing animates the body's height over roughly 250 ms, and the chevron rotates by 180 degrees in sync. The panel has a 1-wide light grey border and corners rounded by 12, and the body must stay clipped to the rounded corners while it animates. The panel starts collapsed. Export a component named `CollapsiblePanel` with props `{ title: string; body: string }`.
